Hello and welcome! I am a PhD student in Industrial Relations at the Centre for Industrial Relations and Human Resources, University of Toronto. My research focuses on transnational labor governance and comparative industrial relations, with a particular interest in the maritime industry.

I examine how international labor rules and institutions interact with governments and firms, and when these interactions strengthen or undermine the enforcement of labor standards. I investigate these questions through studies of port state control inspections under the ILO’s Maritime Labour Convention, transnational collective bargaining, and labor provisions in trade agreements. I also examine how global supply chains shape working conditions in shipping.

My work has been published in ILR Review, Journal of Industrial Relations, and several edited volumes. My research was named a finalist for the 2026 AOM UofSC-Douglas Nigh Best Paper Award and received the 2026 Best Paper Award (First Prize) from the Labor Relations Professional Committee of the China Association for Labor Studies.

My research has been supported by a Regulating for Decent Work Conference Fellowship from the International Labour Organization and a Fieldwork Research Grant from the CRIMT Partnership Project.

Before joining the University of Toronto in 2026, I earned an M.A. (2026) and a B.A. (2024) in Labor Relations from Renmin University of China, with a minor in Public Administration during my undergraduate studies. I was fortunate to receive the Wu Yuzhang Scholarship, the university’s highest student honor, and the China National Scholarship.
